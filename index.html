How to test your webcam

  1. Click “Test my webcam.” Your browser will ask for permission to use your camera — choose Allow.
  2. Check the live preview. If you can see yourself, your camera works. The panel shows the resolution, frame rate and aspect ratio in real time.
  3. Test your microphone. Speak and watch the mic level meter respond so you know your audio is picked up too.
  4. Switch cameras if needed. Use the camera selector to pick a different device, such as an external USB webcam or the rear camera on a phone.

Webcam not working? Common fixes

Permission was blocked

Click the camera or lock icon in the address bar, set the camera to Allow, then reload the page. In Chrome you can also open Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Camera.

Another app is using the camera

Only one app can use the camera at a time. Close Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Skype, OBS or any other video app, then reload this page and try again.

The camera is disabled

On Windows, open Settings → Privacy & security → Camera and make sure camera access is on. On a laptop, check for a physical privacy shutter or a keyboard shortcut that disables the camera.

Nothing happens on click

Camera access needs a secure connection (HTTPS). Make sure the address starts with https://, update your browser to the latest version, and disable extensions that block media devices.

Frequently asked questions

Is this webcam test safe and private?

Yes. The test runs entirely in your browser. Your video and audio never leave your device — nothing is uploaded, stored or recorded on any server.

Why is my webcam not working?

The most common causes are a blocked browser permission, another app already using the camera, the camera being disabled in system settings, or a closed privacy shutter. Close other apps, grant permission when prompted and reload the page.

How do I enable camera permissions in my browser?

Click the camera or lock icon in your browser's address bar, set the camera to Allow, then reload the page. In Chrome you can also go to Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Camera.

Does the webcam test work on phones and tablets?

Yes. It works on Android and iOS in modern browsers such as Chrome and Safari. Use the camera selector to switch between the front and rear cameras.

Which browsers are supported?

All modern browsers are supported, including Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and Opera, on desktop and mobile. The site needs an HTTPS connection to access the camera.
