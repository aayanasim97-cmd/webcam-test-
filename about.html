About WebcamTest

WebcamTest is a free online tool that lets you check whether your webcam and microphone are working — in seconds, and without installing anything. It is designed for anyone who needs to confirm their camera works before a video call, interview, livestream or online exam.

Private by design

Everything happens locally in your browser. The live video is displayed only on your screen; it is never uploaded, stored or recorded. We believe a webcam test should never ask you to send your camera feed to a server, and ours never does.

What you can check

  • Whether your camera produces a live picture
  • The resolution, frame rate and aspect ratio of your camera
  • Whether your microphone picks up sound, using the live level meter
  • Multiple cameras, with a selector to switch between them

Works everywhere

WebcamTest runs in all modern browsers — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and Opera — on desktop, laptop, Android and iOS. No downloads, no sign-up, no cost.
